## The legal note

Clearance applies a creator's own words. It is **not legal advice** and it does not decide what copyright law allows,
such as fair use. The judge is told to decide only from the license and its tiers and to ignore outside law. Every
receipt says this in plain words.

## Honest limits

- **It cannot prove authorship.** Anyone can publish a work and sell licenses for it. Version one shows the creator's
  link and address on every work and receipt, and every work page has a report link. Verified creators, through a
  file on the creator's own domain, are a stretch goal that is not built.
- **A receipt is only as good as the creator behind it.** It proves the creator on record granted this use under
  this version; it cannot prove that creator had the right to.
- **It does not police misuse.** It records permission. A receipt is strong evidence either way in a dispute, but
  nothing on chain stops a use.
- **The judge can miss.** On the held-out cases the deployed judge matched two of three. It answered DENIED where the
  expected answer was UNCLEAR for a newspaper's election explainer under a "no political use" license. The results
  are on [Evaluation results](/docs/more/evaluation), unchanged. UNCLEAR is the safe exit, and the creator keeps the
  last word on it, but a DENIED answer reaches the creator only if the requester writes to them.
- **No ask fee.** The spec suggests creators could set a small ask fee refunded on FREE, against request spam. It is
  not built: a refund would move money inside the judging transaction, which Clearance never does. Each ask costs
  its own network fee.
- **Prices are held for seven days.** After that the requester asks again, and the answer may differ if the license
  changed.
- **A creator price uses a tier.** A creator answering with a price names one of the current license's tiers. To
  offer a new price, amend the license to add a tier first.
- **Studio Next is a test network.** GEN there is test money from a faucet. The contract, answers and receipts are
  real transactions on it, and nothing more.
- **Demo works are fictional.** The five works published by the seed script were written for the demo; their
  creators are invented. See `docs/seed.md` in the repository.

## Stretch goals, not built

Verified creators, an embeddable "Clear to use, ask here" badge, and bundles of works under one license. The receipt
check for platforms is available as a read, described in [Receipt check for platforms](/docs/reference/receipt-check).
