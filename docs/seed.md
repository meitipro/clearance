# The demo works

The five works below were published on the deployed Clearance contract by `scripts/seed.py`, so that the site has
something real to show: licenses in five styles, requests across all four answers, purchases, a creator's answers
and a withdrawal. **The works and their creators are fictional.** Nara Ito, Sami Rahal, Ilse Varga, Oren Dahl and
Lena Okafor are names written for this demo, each published from a test account made for it, and no image, loop,
font or dataset stands behind them. Every answer they received is real: each one came from the deployed judge
through consensus on GenLayer Studio Next, and the record of every transaction is in
[seed.studio-next.md](seed.studio-next.md).

If you are testing Clearance, publish a work of your own or ask about one of these; the site walks you through
getting test GEN.

## Fox in the Reeds

An illustration by Nara Ito. Personal use free with credit, a commercial tier and a merch tier up to 500 units, no
AI training. Amended during the seed to add a large-run tier, so it has two versions.

## Dune Sketches

A photo series by Sami Rahal. Non-commercial and editorial use free with credit, a commercial tier for advertising.

## Tidepool Loops

Music loops by Ilse Varga. Free in your own videos and streams, monetised or not, with credit; no resale, no sample
packs, no AI training; a sync tier for client work.

## Kestrel Type

A font by Oren Dahl. Personal projects free, a commercial tier for web and print, an app tier for embedding.

## Harbor Transit Dataset

A dataset by Lena Okafor. Free for research and teaching with a citation, a commercial tier for analytics, no
redistribution of the raw files.
